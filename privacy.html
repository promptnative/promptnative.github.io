Privacy Policy

PromptNative and PromptNative Publisher · Last updated: 18 November 2026
HomeTerms of Service

1. Who we are

PromptNative ("we") is a short-video channel. PromptNative Publisher ("the app") is our internal tool used only by the PromptNative team to publish our own videos to our own accounts on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok. Contact: promptnative@gmail.com.

2. Who uses the app

Only the PromptNative account owner. The app is not available to the public, and it never signs in to, reads, or posts to any account other than PromptNative's own accounts.

3. Data the app accesses

  • TikTok: when the account owner connects the app through TikTok Login Kit, we receive an access token and basic profile information of the PromptNative TikTok account (open ID, display name, avatar) and the creator settings needed to post (privacy options, interaction settings, maximum video length). We use the scopes user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish only to upload and publish PromptNative's own videos.
  • Other platforms: access tokens for PromptNative's own YouTube channel, Facebook Page and Instagram account, used only to schedule and publish our own videos.
  • Our own content: the video files, titles and captions we created.

We do not collect data about viewers, followers or any other TikTok users. We do not use cookies or analytics on this website.

4. How we use and store data

Data is used only to publish PromptNative's videos and to show the account owner the information TikTok requires before posting. Access tokens are stored encrypted in our automation system's credential store and are never published or shared. Video files are stored in PromptNative's own cloud storage.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal data. Data is sent only to the platform being posted to (for example, the video and caption are sent to TikTok when the account owner chooses to post).

6. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the account owner keeps the app connected. The owner can disconnect the app at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions), which revokes access; we then delete the stored token. To request deletion of any data, email promptnative@gmail.com.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children and is used only by the PromptNative account owner.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
